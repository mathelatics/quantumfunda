Formalizing Quantum Computing in Lean 4

Dependent Types, Unitary Algebras, and Mathlib for Quantum Theory

This monograph develops interactive theorem proving for quantum computing from the foundations of the Calculus of Inductive Constructions to algebraic hierarchies of Hilbert spaces in Mathlib 4. Every chapter pairs mathematical definitions in LaTeX with complete, self-contained Lean 4 formalizations.
