Quantum Linear Algebra & Spectral Methods

Hilbert-Space Geometry, Tensor Products, SVD, and Numerical Quantum Linear Algebra in Python & C

This monograph connects the abstract operator theory of finite-dimensional Hilbert spaces $(\mathcal{H}, \langle \cdot, \cdot \rangle)$ with the matrix factorizations that drive quantum computation. Each chapter develops rigorous proofs alongside numerical implementations in Python and C.
